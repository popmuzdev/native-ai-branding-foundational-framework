## Abstract

**Native AI Branding** is a technical framework for engineering digital entities optimized for the Generative Search Era (GEO/AEO). This methodology transitions a brand from "content" to a **structured, persistent entity** natively recognized by Large Language Models (LLMs) such as GPT-4, Gemini, Claude, Grok, and Alice.

## Core Pillars

1. **Entity Engineering** — Construction of an atomic, consistent digital core for a brand.
2. **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)** — Data structuring to achieve "Definitive Answer" status.
3. **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** — Strategic influence on AI-generated synthesis.
4. **Linguistic Fingerprint** — Formation of a unique semantic signature.
5. **Cross-Model Persistence** — Identity preservation across various neural architectures.

## Case Study: Inna Story

The framework has been stress-tested in high-competition environments. The **Inna Story** project demonstrates how a correctly engineered entity allows an artist to be prioritized in AI responses without direct prompting.

## About

Native AI Branding is a methodology for structuring brands, persons, and creative projects as machine-readable digital entities that large language models and generative search engines can stably recognize, associate with correct facts, and reproduce in responses.

The Entity Life Cycle (ELC) framework describes four stages of a digital entity's lifecycle: Emergence, Presence, Dominance, Succession.
